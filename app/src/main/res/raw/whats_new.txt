## 0.6.9
System now shows the last hour: memory and processor as two lines, and the worst moment named under them, with the app that was using the most memory then. It takes one reading a minute while the TV is on, keeps only the last hour on the TV, and never wakes a sleeping TV.

## 0.6.8
New: System, from the home screen. It shows how hard the TV is working (memory, swap, processor, storage) and which apps use the most memory. Pick one to stop it now, or add a stop-only rule. And a scheduled refresh now never stops the app you're watching: it waits and tries again.

## 0.6.7
Setup can now get Shizuku for you on TVs whose store doesn't offer it, like Google TV. Press Get: Refresher downloads Shizuku from its own GitHub page, checks it really is Shizuku signed by its developer, and opens the TV's installer for you to confirm. It's the only thing Refresher ever downloads.

## 0.6.6
New in Setup: Privacy. It says plainly what Refresher can see and do (nothing leaves the TV). The boot receiver is also no longer reachable by other apps.

## 0.6.5
An app's page now says that changes save as you make them, and shows "✓ Saved" after each one. There's no Save button to press.

## 0.6.4
New in Setup: "Remove Refresher's setup". It says exactly what it will undo, undoes automatic setup, deletes the backup, then offers to uninstall Refresher. It also lists what's left for you if you want everything gone.

## 0.6.3
Setup is simpler: one card says what to have ready, exactly what automatic setup changes, and the two questions the TV will ask. The results are in plain words.

## 0.6.2
Back works cleanly: after a restart returns you to Refresher, Back no longer walks you through copies of screens you'd already left, and Back from the main screen always goes to the TV's home screen.

## 0.6.1
The "Clear its cache too" option from 0.6.0 is gone: Android on this TV doesn't let one app clear another app's cache, so it couldn't work. Nothing was ever deleted.

## 0.6.0
Tried a "Clear its cache too" option (removed again in 0.6.1).

## 0.5.1
These notes: Refresher shows what changed after each update, and any time under Setup › What's new.
All the checks fit on the home screen at once, and Activity and Setup have their own buttons at the top right.

## 0.5.0
Refresher can also restart an app when the TV turns on, for a fresh start each time you watch (at most every 30 minutes). Turn it on per app: "Also when the TV turns on".
After restarting a VPN, Refresher checks it really reconnected and tries once more if it didn't. The log shows how long it took.
Refresher keeps a backup of your apps that survives reinstalling it, and offers to restore it after setup.
A restart never pulls you out of an app you just opened.

## 0.4.0
"Refresh now" ignores repeat presses within a minute, so nothing can keep cutting your VPN.
Tighter security: Refresher now ships as a release build with its own signing key.

## 0.3.1
After a restart, the screen you were on stays in front even if the app opens a screen of its own (like NordVPN's connect screen).

## 0.3.0
A new look: the home screen says straight away whether everything's working, and how to fix it if not.
If Shizuku has stopped, a restart starts it again first. If Refresher still can't stop the app, the log says so instead of reporting success.
Next-run times fit on the app cards at large text sizes.
